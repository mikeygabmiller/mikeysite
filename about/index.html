One person, every car

There's no crew here and no subcontractors. Every car booked with Mikey's Mobile Detailing is detailed by me, start to finish. That's the whole reason the work is consistent across 300+ cars, because you're not rolling the dice on which employee shows up.

It also means I'm the one who answers when something isn't right. If you're not happy with the result, you don't pay. I'd rather redo a car than have a review I have to explain.

Why mobile instead of a shop

A detail takes a few hours. Dropping your car at a shop means arranging a ride there, a ride back, and losing most of a day. I bring everything with me (water, power tools, extraction, polishers, product) and work in your driveway while you get on with your day. You need an outdoor spigot and a power outlet within reach. You don't need to be home; plenty of customers leave the keys and come back to a finished car.

It also means I can work in apartment lots and office parking, which matters around Lynnwood, Everett and Mill Creek where a lot of people have nowhere to wash a car at all.
