The PNW problem

Why Coat a Car Here

If you live around here, you already know what your car deals with. Bare paint takes a beating, and waxing every 3 months barely keeps up. One coating protects it for 2 to 7 years.

Months of rain

Water beads up and rolls off coated paint, taking the dirt with it, so the car stays cleaner between washes.

Winter road grime

Road salt and spray bond to bare clear coat. On a coating, grime releases with a rinse instead of a scrub.

Sap, pollen, droppings

Bird droppings and tree sap wipe away instead of etching in, which is what ruins a clear coat over a spring.

Summer UV

A coating blocks the UV that fades and oxidizes paint, so the color stays deep, not chalky.

Honest take

It's Not a Magic Shield

A coating is the strongest paint protection you can put on a daily driver. Here's what it does and what it doesn't, before you spend the money.

What a coating does

  • Sheds water and dirt. Water beads and rolls off, and grime releases with a rinse.
  • Resists chemicals and UV. Droppings, sap, salt and bug splatter can't bond the same way, and color doesn't fade.
  • Makes washing easier. Less scrubbing means less risk of swirl marks.
  • Keeps the gloss for years, not weeks like wax, and a well-kept car shows better at trade-in.

What it doesn't

  • Stop rock chips. Rocks still chip a coated car.
  • Survive brush washes. Automatic brushes still scratch, and they wear a coating down.
  • Hide swirls. It locks in whatever is on the paint, which is why correction comes first on Pro and Elite.
  • Wash itself. It still needs a hand wash with pH-neutral soap. It just comes clean faster.

Common questions

Before You Book

How long does ceramic coating last?

Two to seven years, depending on the tier. Careful hand washes and staying out of automatic brush washes are what get a coating to the long end.

Is ceramic coating worth it in the Pacific Northwest?

Yes, especially here. Constant rain, road salt, sap and pollen all bond to bare paint. A coating makes water bead off and lets that contamination wipe away, which matters more in our wet climate than in a dry one.

Do I need paint correction before ceramic coating?

Almost always. A coating locks in whatever is on the paint, so swirl marks, oxidation or contamination get sealed in for years. Correcting first means the coating goes on clean paint.

Can ceramic coating be applied at my home?

Yes. I apply coatings in driveways, garages and covered carports across Snohomish County. The car has to stay dry for 24 hours after, so a garage is best, but a covered driveway works too.

How long does the application take?

The coating itself takes 4 to 8 hours. Pro and Elite add paint correction first, and a one-step correction alone is 6 to 8 hours, so plan on more than a day for those. I'll give you the exact days when I quote. The car stays put for 24 hours after to cure.

Do I need to be home?

You need to be home at the start to confirm the work and at the end to walk through aftercare. In between, you're free.

What if it rains?

Application happens in your garage or under cover. The 24-hour cure window also needs dry shelter, so I plan around the weather when you book.

Can you coat a brand-new car?

Yes, and it's the ideal time. Brand-new paint with no swirl marks gets the cleanest coating result, so book it for soon after you pick the car up.

What about wheels and glass?

Wheel face coating and glass coating are included starting at the Standard tier. Brake dust wipes off coated wheels way easier, and rain sheets off coated windshields without needing wipers as much.

Do you detail or coat RVs and trailers?

Yes, RVs, travel trailers and horse trailers, quoted per job. Size, the shape it's in and what it's made of (gelcoat, painted or polished aluminum) change the work too much for one price, so text me a few photos at (425) 600-7897 and tell me what you want done, and I'll give you a real number.
