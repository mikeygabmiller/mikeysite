## 1. Facebook Page (10 min, Facebook app)

You need your own personal Facebook profile; Meta won't let a Page exist
without a person who owns it. The Page shows the business, not your profile.

1. Facebook app → **Menu** (☰) → **Pages** → **Create** (or **Create Page**).
2. If it asks, pick **Public Page** → **Next** → **Get Started**.
3. **Name:** `Mikey's Mobile Detailing` (exactly this, same as Google).
4. **Category:** type `detail` and take the closest match (Car detailing or
   Auto detailing). If nothing matches, `Automotive Service`.
5. **Bio** (93 of 101 characters):
   ```
   Mobile car detailing in your driveway, Snohomish County. Pay after the work, never a deposit.
   ```
   → **Create Page**.
6. **Contact info:**
   - Website: `https://mikeysdetailing.com/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=page`
   - Phone: `(425) 600-7897`
   - Email: `book@mikeysdetailing.com`
7. **Location: leave the street address blank.** If it asks for one, skip it,
   or switch off "show address". Your house never goes on the Page.
8. **Hours:** skip.
9. **Photos:** profile picture `social/posts/profile-picture.jpg`, cover
   `social/posts/facebook-cover.jpg` (Claude can text or email them to you).
10. **Action button:** **Book now** or **Learn more**, linked to
    `https://mikeysdetailing.com/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=page#booking`.
11. WhatsApp: **Skip**. Invite friends: up to you. Notifications: **Done**.
12. **Username** (Page settings): `mikeysdetailing` if it's free.

**Do this too: turn on two-factor login** for your personal Facebook
(Settings → Accounts Center → Password and security → Two-factor
authentication). Whoever gets into your personal account controls the Page,
and Meta's support for small Pages is close to nonexistent.

## 3. Connect Make (5 min, a computer is easiest)

This is the only time Make needs you. It gets permission to post; it never
sees your password.

1. Go to `make.com` and sign in (the account you already have).
2. Left menu → **Connections** → **Create a connection** (may say **Add**).
3. Pick **Instagram for Business (Facebook login)** → **Save**. A Facebook
   window opens.
4. **Continue as Mikey**. If it shows **Edit access** or **Opt in to all
   current and future**, choose the one that includes **Mikey's Mobile
   Detailing** and **@mikeysdetailing_sno**. Leave every permission switched on
   (it's asking to post, read comments and read stats; it can't do anything
   else). **Save** / **Got it**.
5. Do it once more for **Facebook Pages**, same Page.
6. Tell Claude "Make is connected." Claude builds the rest from there and
   sends one test post to Facebook, then deletes it.

If Make ever emails that a connection "expired", it's this same sign-in again,
about a minute.

## 4. Claim Nextdoor (10 min plus a phone call)

Your page already exists, unclaimed:
`https://nextdoor.com/pages/mikeys-mobile-detailing-snohomish-snohomish-wa/`

1. Open that link while signed in to Nextdoor (your personal account is
   fine for claiming; posts will come from the Business Page).
2. Tap **Claim page** (or **Claim this business**). Choose **professional
   business**, not "neighbor for hire".
3. **Before you tap verify, switch off call screening.** Your business line
   answers with "press 1 to reach Mikey", and Nextdoor's verification call is
   a robot that won't press 1, so it would get hung up on. In the dashboard:
   **More → Settings → Auto follow-ups & calls → Call screening**, switch it
   off. Then let Nextdoor call `(425) 600-7897`, answer on your cell, and type
   the code it reads out. **Switch call screening back on right after**, or
   the spam calls come back.
4. If the call doesn't work, Nextdoor asks for a document instead: a
   business bank statement (account number blacked out), an IRS EIN letter or
   a business license. You don't have a license, so it'd be the bank statement
   or the EIN letter if you have one. Tell Claude if it gets stuck here.
5. Once it's yours:
   - Display name: `Mikey's Mobile Detailing` (drop "- Snohomish")
   - Email: `book@mikeysdetailing.com`
   - Description: the **500 block** in `outreach/DIRECTORIES.md`
   - Reach: pick the twelve towns' neighborhoods, **not** the default radius,
     which runs into Lynnwood and Edmonds
