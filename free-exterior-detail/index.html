Quick Answers

Is the exterior detail really free?

Yes. Book an interior detail, from $200, and the complete exterior detail — a $160 service — is added at no charge. You pay the interior price and nothing else. There's no coupon to enter and no deposit.

How do I claim it?

Text or call (425) 600-7897 and say you want the free exterior deal. I'll ask what you drive and roughly what shape it's in, give you an exact price, and book a day. That's the entire process.

Where do you do the detail?

Wherever the car is. I'm fully mobile and cover Snohomish, Everett, Lake Stevens, Mill Creek, Monroe, Bothell, Marysville, Duvall and the surrounding area — roughly 25 miles from Snohomish, WA. There's no charge for the drive.

What do you need from me?

An outdoor water spigot and a power outlet, and access to the vehicle. You don't need to be home — a lot of people leave the keys and go to work.

When do I pay?

After. We walk around the car together when I'm done and if anything isn't right I fix it before I take a dollar. You don't pay until you love it.

Does it work on trucks and big SUVs?

Yes. Larger vehicles price higher on the interior because they take longer, but the free exterior applies exactly the same way. Trucks and family SUVs are most of what I detail. Full details on the deals page →
