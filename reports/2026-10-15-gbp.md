## What Windsor can and can't do on the profile

- **Can:** reply to reviews; edit any post's text, photo or button,
  scheduled ones included; make a plain update post; set the description,
  website, primary phone, services, service area, attributes (social links,
  booking link), hours, photos.
- **Can't:** the name, additional phones, an Offer's title and terms,
  deleting a post, making an Offer post.
- **Don't, without Mikey saying so in those words:** the address or the
  categories (either can unpublish the listing until it re-verifies), open
  or closed status.
- **Services take one flat price with no "starting at",** so the ranges go in
  each service's description, never the price field
  (`outreach/DIRECTORIES.md`: never a single flat price for a range).
