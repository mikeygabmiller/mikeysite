# AEO pass, 2026-10-07

The October scoreboard (`reports/2026-10-scoreboard.md`) left two problems for
answer engines: Mikey is named on 6 of 15 town prompts but **0 of 10 question
prompts**, even where his own page was the source, and the answers that do
name him quote **old prices and times** ($160+ interior, $260+ full, "3-4
hours", "38+ reviews") that the site retired weeks ago. This pass went after
both: where the old facts still live, and making every answer on the site carry
his name.

## 1. Off the site: where the old facts come from (Mikey's to fix)

All checked live on 2026-10-07. In order of how much they matter.

### Google Business Profile: still says "from $200"

Google's own public page for the profile lists, under Services:

> **Auto detailing, From $200.00.** Full interior/exterior detail starting at just $200.

A Full Detail is $369–$449. GBP is the source AI engines lean on most for a
local business, and Bing Places copies it, so this one matters more than
anything on the website. The same page also shows:

- **A street address.** `outreach/DIRECTORIES.md` says it's hidden. It isn't.
- **The old agency-voice description** ("We offer a comprehensive range...").
- **5.0 from 39 reviews.** The site says 41 (CLAUDE.md, `site-stats.js`).
  Google may have filtered two. If the profile itself says 39, the site's
  number has to come down to match: engines cross-check the two.

**Fix (15 minutes, on a computer, signed in to the profile):**

1. Search Google for "my business", open **Edit profile**.
2. **Services** (or Products & services): delete "Auto detailing / from $200".
   Add the five from the **Services** table in `outreach/DIRECTORIES.md`
   (Exterior $199–$279, Interior $249–$329, Full $369–$449, Paint Correction
   from $400, Ceramic Coating from $500). Where a field takes one number, use
   the low end with "starting at".
3. **Description**: paste the **500-character block** from
   `outreach/DIRECTORIES.md`. Not the 750 one: Google's rules for this field
   bar links and copy that centers on prices, and the 750 has both. The
   prices belong in Services.
4. **Location**: switch off "show business address to customers" and set the
   service area to the twelve towns.
5. Read the review count on the profile and tell the next session the number.

Do this **before** importing into Bing Places (DIRECTORIES.md step 2), or Bing
copies the $200.

## 2. On the site (shipped with this report)

### The FAQ schema can't drift any more

23 pages carried FAQ schema (the JSON-LD that Google and AI engines read)
that didn't match the FAQ on the page: questions only the schema asked, and
answers only the schema gave. Some of those hidden answers were wrong:

| Page | The hidden or drifted answer said | Facts table |
|---|---|---|
| About | serves 8 towns | 12 |
| Cost | "SUVs and trucks cost 25-50% more" | +$40 SUV or pickup, +$80 van or 3-row |
| Cost | "a mobile detail takes 2-5 hours" | full detail 3–5 |
| Best time | "a full detail every 3-4 months, 3-4 times a year" | Mikey's April post: every 4–6 months |
| Paint correction | two-step "a day and a half to two days", multi-stage "two full days" | multi-stage 1–2 days |

`tools/build-entity-graph.py` now **builds the FAQPage from the visible FAQ**
on every page (answer-first box, `.faq-item`, `details.faq2-item`, or `h3`
pairs under a "Common Questions" `h2`). A question that only lived in the
schema is now either on the page (the About page, the ceramic page, the four
guides and the Polish Test got visible FAQ sections) or gone. `check-site.py`
fails if a visible answer changes without a rerun.

Most AI crawlers strip `<script>` and read only the visible text, so the
answers that were schema-only were invisible to them. Now they're on the page.

### Recrawl signals

- **sitemap.xml**: 31 of 43 URLs said 2026-08-01, though the 10-05 audit had
  changed them. All 43 now carry the day the page last changed, from git
  (`tools/sitemap-lastmod.py`), and `check-site.py` fails if one falls behind.
  This is what Google uses to decide what to recrawl.
- **IndexNow**: a key file at the site root and `tools/indexnow.py`, which
  tells Bing (the index ChatGPT search and Copilot read) which URLs changed.
  See the PR for whether the first submission went through.
