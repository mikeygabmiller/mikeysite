Impression share went *up* while impressions collapsed, which means Google
thinks there were far fewer eligible searches, not that he was outbid. That
usually means the campaign's targeting got narrower: keywords paused or
switched to exact match, the location or ad schedule cut down, or an ad
disapproved. Windsor can't see the change history. **Google Ads → the
campaign → Change history, from Sep 28 to Oct 3** will show what changed.

Some days spent close to 3x the $10 budget (Oct 3: $28.77 for 3 clicks).
Google allows up to 2x on a day and evens it out over the month, so that's
normal, but those three clicks cost $9.59 each.

**The 8 leads, by day:** Sep 12, 17, 18, 21, two on Sep 26, two halves on
Sep 27 and 28, and Sep 30. Saturdays had 3 of the 8; Tuesdays had none.
Most came 8 AM to noon.

**Devices:** 192 of 221 clicks were on a phone (7 of 8 leads).

## Negative keywords to add (Mikey's call, in Google Ads)

Add these at the campaign level as **phrase** negatives. Each one comes from a
search term in this account that spent money ("how to" from the old campaign:
moss removal, DIY paint correction); none of them would block someone looking
for a detail.

```

fluid film
rv

```

Before adding "car wash": "car wash with interior cleaning" showed 3
conversions on the old campaign (probably page loads, see above). If he'd
rather keep that kind of search, use **exact** negatives for "car
washes near me" and "hand carwash" instead of the phrase "car wash".

**Don't negative his own name.** "mikeys mobile detailing" cost $2.53 for a
lead. Brand clicks are cheap and stop a competitor bidding on his name from
taking them.

## Three things to check in Google Ads

1. **Change history, Sep 28 to Oct 3.** Whatever changed there is why the
   campaign went from 8 leads to none.
2. **The Same Day ad group's ads.** If any headline says same day, today, or
   "book today", change it. The calendar never offers same day and a
   customer who clicks for it finds tomorrow at the earliest.
3. **Conversions → Summary.** Primary should be calls and the quote/booking
   only. `qqc_submission` (the quote calculator, from GA4) is set as secondary
   right now. **Leave it secondary** (corrected 2026-10-16): `site-stats.js`
   already sends every quote to Ads directly (label `13rMCOG9vfQcEKTSz-U-`),
   so a primary GA4 copy would count each quote twice. Muse's checklist for
   all of this is `reports/2026-10-16-muse-ads-conversions.md`. "Local
   actions" (directions, other engagements) should stay secondary: they made
   the campaign look like it had 23 more results than it did.
