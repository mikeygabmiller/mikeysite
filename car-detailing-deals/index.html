So What's the Catch?

Fair question. A free $160 service sounds like the setup for an upsell, so here's exactly how it works and why I can afford to do it.

I'm already at your house. The expensive part of mobile detailing isn't the soap, it's the drive, the setup, the water, the power and the pack-down. That overhead is the same whether I clean the inside of your car or the whole thing. Once my canopy is up and my hoses are run, adding the exterior costs me time and product, not another trip. That's the entire economics of this offer.

I'd rather have you than an ad. I'm one guy, not a franchise with a marketing budget. Every dollar a big detailing chain spends on ads to get one new customer, I'd rather spend on your paint. You get a full detail for the price of an interior, and I get someone in Snohomish County who has actually seen my work instead of just read about it.

The real catch is capacity. I detail every car myself — there's no crew. That means a handful of these a week, not unlimited. When the week's spots are gone, they're gone until the next one.

What it is not

It's not a stripped-down "express" version of the exterior detail. It's not contingent on buying a ceramic coating. There's no membership to join, no card on file, and nothing auto-renews. You book an interior, I do the outside too, you pay the interior price.

Common questions

Detailing Deals FAQ

What people ask before they claim it.

Do you offer car detailing deals or specials?

Yes. The standing offer is a free exterior detail — a $160 service — with any interior detail booked in Snohomish County or north King County, WA. You pay the interior price, from $200, and get a complete interior and exterior detail. There is no coupon code and nothing to print; you just tell me you want it when you book.

Is there a coupon code or a printable coupon?

No, and you don't need one. Text or call (425) 600-7897 and say you want the free exterior deal — that's the whole redemption process. Skipping coupon codes is deliberate: it means nobody misses the discount because they forgot to type something in.

Do you run a Groupon for car detailing?

No. Groupon takes a large cut of the ticket, which forces detailers to shrink the service to stay profitable — that's why voucher details are so often a quick wash with add-on charges waiting at the counter. I'd rather give that money to you directly as a free exterior detail and keep the service at full size. You also don't pay anything up front here, so there's no voucher to lose or let expire.

What's the catch with a free exterior detail?

Capacity. I detail every car personally, so only a few of these open up each week. Beyond that there's genuinely no catch: no membership, no card on file, no requirement to buy a coating, and no stripped-down version of the exterior service. The reason it's affordable for me is that the drive, setup, water and pack-down are already paid for by the interior booking — adding the outside costs me time and product, not a second trip.

How much does car detailing cost in Snohomish County?

Interior detailing runs $200–$280, exterior detailing $160–$240, and a full detail $299–$379, priced on vehicle size and condition. With this offer an interior booking gets you the exterior free, so a $360 full detail lands at the interior price. There's a full breakdown on the detailing cost guide.

Is cheap car detailing worth it?

Cheap usually means less work, not a better price — a $79 "detail" is normally a wash and a vacuum, with clay bar, extraction and paint protection stripped out. The thing to compare is the service list, not the headline number. This offer isn't a cheaper service, it's the full service with one half removed from the bill.

Can I get the deal on a truck, SUV or minivan?

Yes. Large vehicles price higher on the interior side because they take longer, but the free exterior applies the same way. Trucks and family SUVs are most of what I detail.

Do I have to be home during the detail?

No. I need access to the vehicle, an outdoor water spigot and a power outlet. Plenty of clients leave the keys and go to work. Payment happens after the job, so nothing changes hands before you've seen the car.

How long is the offer available?

It runs weekly with a limited number of spots, and it's been reopening each week. If the current week is full I'll tell you straight away and put you in the next one — nobody loses the deal for calling on the wrong day.

What if it rains?

This is western Washington, so I'm set up for it. A pop-up canopy handles most weather. For heavy rain or a vehicle too tall for the canopy we'll reschedule, and there's never a fee for a weather cancellation.
