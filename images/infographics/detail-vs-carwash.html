Eight things a car wash can't do

No matter how many times you go
  • ×Clean your carpets, seats, dash and vents
  • ×Clay off tree sap, tar and rail dust
  • ×Pull pet hair out of the upholstery
  • ×Polish out swirl marks or oxidation
  • ×Lift coffee stains, food spills or odors
  • ×Lay down real wax or sealant protection
  • ×Actually clean your wheels — those just get rinsed
  • ×Come to your driveway while you stay put

So which one, today?

Run it through the wash when…

You want salt or mud off the paint fast · you're keeping an already-clean car presentable · it's the in-between week before your next detail.

Book the detail when…

The interior needs real work — kids, pets, job sites · the paint looks dull or feels rough · you're selling or trading it in · it's been 6+ months.

The smart combo

Most of my Clean Club members do both: a detail every one to three months, and a touchless wash in between when the weather gets it. Best of both worlds, and the car never gets far gone.
