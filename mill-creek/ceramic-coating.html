Common Questions

Mill Creek Ceramic FAQ

Do you apply ceramic coating in Mill Creek?

Yes. I serve Mill Creek, including Town Center, the Country Club area, and nearby neighborhoods. There is no travel fee within my service area, and my prices are the same in every town. I confirm the parking space and access before the appointment.

How much does ceramic coating cost?

Ceramic coating starts at $500. Standard starts at $500, Pro at $850, and Elite at $1,300. The final quote depends on the vehicle, paint condition, and preparation needed. Send me the year, make, model, and a few paint photos so I can discuss the right option.

What do I need to provide?

I need access to an outdoor water spigot and a power outlet, plus enough room to work around the vehicle. Tell me whether you have a garage, covered driveway, or open parking space. I will confirm whether the location and weather are suitable for the coating.

Will my HOA allow the appointment?

Please check your HOA or property rules before booking. Mill Creek neighborhoods can have restrictions on driveway work, parking, or water use. Send me any requirements that affect the job so we can plan the setup before I arrive.

Does ceramic coating remove scratches?

No. Coating protects the prepared surface; it does not remove scratches or make the car scratchproof. Paint correction is a separate part of the preparation when needed. I look at the paint first and explain what can reasonably be improved.

How much time should I set aside?

The schedule depends on the prep and coating selected. One-step paint correction takes 6–8 hours; multi-stage correction takes 1–2 days. Coating application and curing need to be planned around that work. I will confirm the total schedule and product-specific cure instructions before the appointment.

How do I look after a coated car?

Keep up with careful maintenance washes and avoid automatic brush washes. A coating still needs cleaning, and bird droppings or other contamination should be removed promptly. I explain the first-wash timing and ongoing care for the product used on your car.
