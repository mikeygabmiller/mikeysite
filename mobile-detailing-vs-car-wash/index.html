Mobile Detailing vs Car Wash

A drive-thru wash takes 5 minutes. A real detail takes hours. Here's the honest difference and which one you actually need.

What is the difference between mobile detailing and a car wash?

A drive-thru car wash is a 5–10 minute exterior rinse for $10–$25. A full mobile detail is 3–5 hours of hands-on work, inside and out, for $369–$449, covering interior stains, pet hair, oxidation and leather (an exterior or interior on its own starts at $199). A wash keeps a clean car clean weekly; a detail resets a dirty one every 4 to 6 months. I'm Mikey, I run Mikey's Mobile Detailing in Snohomish, WA, and plenty of my customers use both.

I'm not going to pretend a car wash is useless, it has a place. But I'll tell you straight up when one is fine and when you're wasting your money.

Side-By-Side

Drive-Thru Car Wash

  • Time: 5-10 minutes
  • Cost: $10-25
  • What it does: Exterior rinse
  • What it misses: Interior, wheels, deep dirt
  • Paint risk: Possible swirl marks from brushes
  • Frequency: Weekly is fine

Mobile Detailing

  • Time: 3-5 hours for a full detail, 1-2 for an exterior
  • Cost: $199-449
  • What it does: Full inside & out
  • What it covers: Stains, pet hair, oxidation, leather
  • Paint risk: None, proper hand wash technique
  • Frequency: Every 4-6 months

What A Car Wash Can't Do

  • Clean your interior (carpets, seats, dashboard, vents)
  • Remove pet hair embedded in upholstery
  • Get rid of coffee stains, food spills, or odors
  • Clay bar contaminants like tree sap, tar, or rail dust
  • Polish out swirl marks or oxidation
  • Protect your paint with real wax or sealant (the "wax" option at a wash is mostly water with shine additive)
  • Clean your wheels properly, those are basically just rinsed

What A Detail Can't Do

  • Be done in 5 minutes
  • Cost $15
  • Be repeated every week (you don't need to)

When To Use Each

Use a car wash when:

  • You need a quick rinse between proper details
  • You drove through mud or salt and want it off the paint fast
  • You're keeping the car presentable on a daily basis

Book a mobile detail when:

  • The interior needs actual work, kids, pets, job sites, road trips
  • The paint looks dull, has swirl marks, or feels rough
  • You're prepping to sell or trade in the car
  • You bought a used car and want to start clean
  • It's been 6+ months since the last real cleaning
The Smart Combo That's how the Clean Club works: a detail with me every 4 or 8 weeks, and a touchless wash in between if you want one.

The Convenience Factor

Here's the thing people forget, a drive-thru means YOU drive there, wait in line, and drive home. A mobile detail means I show up to your driveway. You don't move. For a 2-hour service, that convenience is real.

The Quality Factor

I use professional clay bars, dual-action polishers, hot water extractors, and proper microfiber technique. A car wash uses recycled water and rotating brushes. Both clean, but they don't clean the same way and they don't produce the same result.

Common Questions

Is mobile detailing worth the cost?

It's worth it when you need real cleaning: stains, pet hair, oxidation, or interior grime a car wash can't touch. For a quick exterior refresh between details, a car wash is fine. Most cars want a full detail every 4 to 6 months, with washes in between.

Will a car wash damage my paint?

Automated washes with brushes leave swirl marks and fine scratches over time. Touchless washes are gentler but don't clean as well. When I hand wash during a detail, I use two buckets and microfiber so the wash itself doesn't scratch the paint.
