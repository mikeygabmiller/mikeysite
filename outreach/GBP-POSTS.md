## How to post

Checked 2026-10-05 against Google's help page ("Create & manage posts on your
Business Profile") and current guides. Google moves these buttons around; if a
label doesn't match, look for the nearest one.

**On a computer (easiest, and you can schedule all eight in one sitting):**

1. Sign in to the Google account that owns the profile and search Google for
   **Mikey's Mobile Detailing** (or "my business"). Your profile's edit panel
   shows at the top of the results.
2. Click **Add update** (on some accounts it's under **Promote**, or
   **Posts → Add post**).
3. Pick the type: **Add update** for posts 1, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7, **Add offer**
   for posts 2 and 8.
4. **Photo:** click **Add photos** and pick the file named in the post, from
   `social/photos/` in this repo. JPG, at least 400 x 300.
5. **Text:** paste the post text from the box below into **Description**.
6. **Button** (updates only): click **Add button**, pick the one the post
   names, and for **Book** paste the link. **Call now** uses the number on your
   profile, so check that it's (425) 600-7897 before you use it.
7. **Schedule:** turn on **Schedule this post** and pick the Monday of that
   week, around 8 AM. If your account doesn't show the switch yet, post each
   one on its Monday instead.
8. Click **Post** (or **Schedule**).

**On your phone (Google Maps app):** tap your profile picture (top right) →
**Your Business Profiles** → Mikey's Mobile Detailing → **Promote** (some
phones say **Add update**) → **Add update** or **Add offer**. Same fields as
above: photo first, then description, then the button.

**Offer posts are different.** Google asks for an **offer title**, a **start
date** and an **end date**, and it puts a **View offer** button on the post by
itself. There's no Add button step. Under **Add more details** (or **Additional
details**) paste the **Link to redeem offer** and the **Terms and conditions**
given in the post. Leave **Coupon code** empty: the offer has no code word.

**Three Google rules that matter here:**

- **No phone number in the text.** Google's post policy removes or rejects posts
  with a phone number in the description ("phone stuffing"). That's why none of
  these posts writes the number. People reach you through the **Call now**
  button, which uses the verified number on the profile.
- **1,500 characters at most.** Every post below says its count (line breaks included); all are well
  under, and the first two lines are the ones that show before "More".
- **Only the most recent post shows on the profile** by default; older ones
  move to the Updates tab. That's another reason for one a week, not eight on
  one day.

## 3. Week of Oct 26 · Update · Leaves and sap

**Photo:** `social/photos/lexus-side.jpg` (Lexus on gravel under big trees)
**Button:** Book → `https://mikeysdetailing.com/#booking`

```
If you park under the firs and maples out in Woodinville, Duvall or Granite Falls, this one's for you.

Wet leaves are more than a mess. They hold water against the paint, and the tannins in them leave brown stains that look like a shadow of the leaf once it blows off. Sap is worse: it hardens on cold paint and pulls the clear coat with it if you scrape at it.

What I'd do this week:

1. Brush leaves off with your hand or a soft brush. Don't let them sit through a week of rain, and never wipe dry ones across the paint.
2. Clear the cowl, the gap under the windshield wipers. Leaves rot in there and plug the drains, and that water ends up on your floor.
3. Leave sap alone until you can soften it. Hand sanitizer on a microfiber, let it sit a minute, then lift. No fingernails, no scraper.
4. Check the sunroof drains and door bottoms on anything that sits outside all winter.

If the stains or sap have already set, that's what my exterior work is for. Tap Book and the quote shows your price in 60 seconds.
```

The clocks went back, and now the drive home from work is in the dark and usually in the rain.

Most people blame the weather when night driving gets hard. A lot of the time it's the glass. Here's what to check:
